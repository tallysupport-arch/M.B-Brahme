How to visit Math

Coming from Mumbai :

Take the Mumbai Pune Expressway,
After 7 Kms, take left from Shedung phata,
You will come to Old Mumbai Pune Road,
Come straight upto Chowk Phata ( before khopoli )
Take left from Chowk phata towards Karjat
You may stop over for refreshments at Raj Cottage ( Veg )
Come to Junction of Matheran & Karjat,
Go Straight towards karjat town,
Take the Karjat Murbad Road,
You have to cross the Ulhas River Bridge,
After about 7 kms you come to Kadav Village,
Before Kadav Village, take a right on TATA POWER ROAD,
After about 1.5 Kms, you come to SWAMI MATH.

Coming from Pune :

Take the Mumbai Pune Expressway,
After crossing Lonavla you come to Khalapur Toll Plaza
Take right to come to Old Mumbai Pune Road,
Come straight upto Chowk Phata
Take right from Chowk phata towards Karjat
You may stop over for refreshments at Raj Cottage ( Veg )
Come to Junction of Matheran & Karjat,
Go Straight towards karjat town,
Take the Karjat Murbad Road,
You have to cross the Ulhas River Bridge,
After about 7 kms you come to Kadav Village,
Before Kadav Village, take a right on TATA POWER ROAD,
After about 1.5 Kms, you come to SWAMI MATH.

Coming from Nashik :

Take the Mumbai Nashik Expressway,
At Murbad, take left to come to Karjat
Take the Murbad Karjat Road
Come upto Village Kashele ( near Bhimashankar )
About 3 Kms after Kashele is village Kadav
After Kadav Village, take a left on TATA POWER ROAD,
After about 1.5 Kms, you come to SWAMI MATH.
